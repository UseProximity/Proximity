## 10. Lease Check

Lease Check is currently available on the website only. You upload a lease and an AI model

**Lease Check is not legal advice, and Proximity is not a law firm.** No attorney-client
relationship is created by using it. The analysis is generated by an AI model, it makes
mistakes, and it may miss something important or flag something that is fine. Do not rely on

university's student legal services office before you sign.

The lease file you upload is deleted from our storage as soon as the analysis finishes; we
keep only the output. See Privacy Policy §1.7 for exactly what is and is not retained.
